

# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

A complete end-to-end Data Analytics project that leverages **Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze customer shopping behavior, uncover business insights, and provide data-driven recommendations.

This project demonstrates the full analytics workflow:

**Data Cleaning → Feature Engineering → Database Analysis → Business Insights → Dashboard Visualization**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze customer shopping behavior using transactional purchase data and identify patterns related to:

* Customer demographics
* Product preferences
* Revenue generation
* Subscription behavior
* Discount effectiveness
* Customer loyalty
* Age-group spending trends

The analysis was performed on **3,900 customer transactions** across multiple product categories.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Gender-Based Revenue

* Male customers generated significantly higher revenue than female customers.

### Subscription Behavior

* Non-subscribers contributed the majority of revenue.
* Opportunity exists to improve subscription conversion.

### Customer Loyalty

* Most customers fall into the Loyal segment.
* Loyalty programs can further increase retention.

### Product Performance

Top-rated products include:

* Gloves
* Sandals
* Boots
* Hat
* Skirt

### Discount Analysis

Products most dependent on discounts:

* Hat
* Sneakers
* Coat
* Sweater
* Pants
